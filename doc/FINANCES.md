<br/>

When someone on the team needs a part for the robot, here's how we get it ordered:

1. **Pick the part**: Build or software team figures out what part or sensor we need.
2. **Double check specs**: Make sure it fits the robot, runs on the right voltage, and is FTC legal.
3. **Ask Iestyn**: Check with Iestyn to see if we have room in the budget.
4. **Mentor approval**: Get the thumbs up from Alison or Eben.
5. **Get it ordered**: Order through the college.
6. **Log receipt**: Give the receipt to Iestyn so he can update the sheet!
